# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DecisionLane welcomes respectful participation from contributors and users of different backgrounds and experience levels.
We expect people to work together in a way that makes the project safe and useful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions.
- Give specific technical feedback and assume good faith while seeking clarification.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Protect private information shared during collaboration.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help resolve their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or reviews.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies to project spaces and to public conduct when someone is representing DecisionLane.
To report a concern privately, email the maintainer at `khanhnguyen.trq@gmail.com` with relevant links or context.
Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.
The maintainer will review reports, limit access to the information where practical, and take proportionate action, which may include removing content or restricting participation.
Reports involving the maintainer can be raised through GitHub's abuse reporting tools.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may ask for a correction, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant, depending on the conduct and its impact.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is unacceptable.
